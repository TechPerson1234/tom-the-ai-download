How to Use Tom The AI (Beginner's Guide)

Follow these simple steps to get started with Tom

1

Visit the Main Website

Click the big "Visit Tom The AI Main Website" button above or go directly to https://techperson1234.github.io/TOM-The-AI-Wen/. That's where the AI chat lives.

2

Choose a Persona

Once on the main site, you'll see a sidebar or chips to select a persona like "Coder" or "Researcher". Pick the one that fits your task. The AI adapts its style and knowledge accordingly.

3

Start Chatting

Type your message in the input box and press Enter. Tom will respond quickly. You can ask for code, analysis, creative ideas, or just have a friendly conversation.

4

Explore Themes & Memory

Toggle between Dark and Galaxy themes with the button in the top bar. Your chat history is saved locally in your browser, so you can pick up where you left off.

5

Download a Native App (Optional)

If you prefer a dedicated app, scroll down to the Downloads section here or on the main site. Get the IPA for iOS, APK for Android, EXE for Windows, DMG for macOS, or AppImage for Linux.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tom The AI really free?
Yes, completely free. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no hidden costs. It is community-shared and hosted on Github.
What does "~30% chance of breaking" mean?
Tom is a work in progress. About 30% of the time, especially on very complex or unusual requests, it might return an error or an unhelpful response. This is a known quirk and the team is always working to improve reliability.
How do I switch between Dark and Galaxy themes?
Use the "Theme" button in the top bar of the main website, or the toggle on this portal page. Your choice is saved in your browser.
Does Tom collect my personal data?
Only minimal, anonymous usage data is collected to help improve the AI. No personal conversations are stored on external servers. Local history is kept only on your device.
What is the difference between the web version and the native apps?
The native apps (IPA, APK, EXE, DMG, AppImage) provide a dedicated, installable experience on each platform, often with better integration and offline caching. The core AI features remain the same.
Can I use Tom on my iPhone?
Yes. There is an iOS IPA and a mobile configuration profile that adds a bookmark to your home screen. However, support for iOS/Safari may be limited compared to other platforms.

Community & Transparency

Built for the people, shared by the people

Tom The AI is not a corporate product. It is a closed-source project that is shared freely with the community. The team behind Tom believes in accessibility — no ads, no paywalls, no tracking beyond basic improvement metrics.
